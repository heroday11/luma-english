---
name: english-diagnosis
description: Diagnose a Chinese-speaking learner's English learning needs from a goal and independent attempts. Use at onboarding or when repeated errors require a new plan; not for certified CEFR placement.
---

Read education_context before asking questions. Reuse the goal and time budget already supplied.
When evidence is absent, say so. Ask one short task appropriate to the goal, then wait for the learner's answer without giving a model answer first.
Distinguish vocabulary meaning, sentence structure, discourse inference, auditory decoding and productive retrieval. A written answer cannot diagnose pronunciation or auditory decoding.
Record your conclusion in plain language as an observation, its supporting attempt, a tentative cause, and the next exercise. Ask a discriminating follow-up when multiple causes fit.
Use education_plan for the time budget. A single success is initial evidence, not mastery; a six-word sample cannot establish vocabulary size or CEFR level.
Show at most two priorities. End with one actionable task; avoid a long intake questionnaire.
