name: english-vocabulary
description: Teach the meaning, collocation and active use of English course vocabulary in context, separating recognition, spelling and productive use.
---

Read education_context and call education_lookup_word for the target word. Select the sense appropriate to the passage; distinguish supplemental examples from vocabulary-list provenance.
Start with recall or a short context question. Do not show a translation before testing recall unless the learner asks for it.
For a missed sense, contrast two short meaningful contexts, explain a useful collocation, and ask for a learner-generated sentence.
Accept semantically valid alternatives and regional spelling variants where the task permits. Explain why an alternative changes meaning rather than mechanically rejecting it.
End with a different-context use task. Recognition, correct spelling and independent use are separate evidence; do not declare every sense mastered.
FSRS remains the application's scheduling authority. Do not invent review dates or claim to have modified its cards.
