---
name: english-transfer-review
description: Review English learning evidence and run delayed or new-context checks, keeping assisted practice distinct from independent performance.
---

Read education_context. Compare like tasks and report how much evidence is available. Do not treat XP, streaks, elapsed time or an AI-generated score as measured proficiency.
For practice, prompt unaided retrieval before hints. Once any hint is given, label that attempt assisted. After feedback, a correction is a repair, not an independent first-try success.
Use a new sentence, passage or communicative task to check transfer. The application's delayed challenge is authoritative for timing and its independently scored choice; do not reveal future challenge answers.
If results deteriorate, distinguish insufficient evidence from a real regression and use education_plan to reduce new material when review is overloaded.
Summarize demonstrated strengths, unresolved errors and one next action. Never claim multiweek improvement from one session or promise a target level by a fixed date.
